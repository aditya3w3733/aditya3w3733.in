I'm Aditya, a Software Engineer at Propheus. I work with cloud infrastructure & AI systems. This is my corner of the internet.

## Why This Blog Exists

My father always writes these wonderfully amusing novels, stories, poems etc. I always wondered if I could write something too. Can I write something on my own? I thought I wouldn't have anything interesting to write about or rather, that no one would read what I have to say.

But I do articulate my thoughts. I write research notes, programming ideas, chess variations, and personal experiences in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md). I write film reviews on [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/Aditya3w3733/). I even leave long Reddit comments about things that interest me. But for a long time, I wanted to write more publicly about software, tools I use, LLMs, things I learn, chess, films and much more.

Then I stumbled upon [this blog post](https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/start-a-blog) that finally pushed me to stop overthinking and just start.


## What I'll Write About

- **Engineering**: Cloud infrastructure, tools I use
- **AI & LLMs**: Experiments, learnings, ideas
- **Personal**: Productivity, journaling, what I'm learning
- **Chess**: Tactics, games, strategy
- **Films**: Reviews and thoughts on cinema

## Creating vs. Consuming

For the past year, I've been on a journey to reclaim my attention.

I quit social media - Instagram & Twitter specifically, exactly one year ago and started [interstitial journaling](https://nesslabs.com/interstitial-journaling), which helped me procrastinate less and keep track of things daily.

This blog is part of that journey. It's long-term dopamine against short-term scrolling. Creating rather than consuming. It's thinking out loud. It's learning in public.

## What's Next

I don't know if anyone will read this. That's okay I guess. Writing exercises my brain better than scrolling ever did. Anything I create will be better than mindless consumption.

If you're reading this: welcome. Let's see where this goes.
